What skin biorevitalization is

Skin biorevitalization, sometimes called a skin booster treatment, uses hyaluronic-acid-based solutions to hydrate the skin, improve its luminosity and support collagen synthesis. It gives subtle improvements by restoring the skin's own hydration. It improves skin quality and suppleness and may help delay visible signs of ageing.

Biorevitalization vs. dermal fillers

Both treatments use hyaluronic acid, but with different aims. Biorevitalization focuses on hydration and skin quality. Dermal fillers restore lost volume, soften wrinkles and fill hollow areas and deep folds. Read about dermal fillers in our services.

Who it may suit

Biorevitalization may suit adults who want to improve the hydration, luminosity and suppleness of their skin with subtle changes. Whether it is suitable for you is decided at your consultation with Dr. Koerge, who has more than 20 years of experience in wrinkle treatments and non-surgical facial rejuvenation. Frequently Asked Questions

Biorevitalization or filler: what's the difference?

Biorevitalization uses hyaluronic-acid-based solutions to hydrate the skin and improve its luminosity and quality. Dermal fillers use hyaluronic acid to restore lost volume, soften wrinkles and fill hollow areas and deep folds. Dr. Koerge will advise you at your consultation which approach suits your goals.

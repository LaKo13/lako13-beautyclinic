What skin biorevitalization is

Biorevitalization uses hyaluronic-acid-based solutions to hydrate the skin, improve luminosity, and support collagen synthesis. It gives subtle improvements by restoring the skin's own hydration and may help delay visible signs of ageing. The treatment focuses on improving skin quality and suppleness on the face, neck, and décolleté.

Biorevitalization vs. dermal fillers

Biorevitalization aims to improve hydration and skin quality, while dermal fillers restore volume, fill wrinkles, and enhance contours such as lips and cheeks. Both treatments use hyaluronic acid but serve different purposes. If you are considering fillers as well, Dr. Koerge can discuss this during your consultation.

Who it may suit

Biorevitalization may be suitable for adults seeking to improve skin hydration and quality. Suitability is decided at a personal consultation with Dr. Koerge, where your skin condition, medical history, and aesthetic goals are assessed.

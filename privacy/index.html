Website privacy

How this website works and how to contact us about privacy.

Contact

For questions about this website or an enquiry you have sent, contact Beauty Clinic Mallorca at info@beautyclinicmallorca.com or +34 971 707 725. Our address is Calle Arquitecto Francisco Casas 17, Plaza Bendinat, Local B10, 07181 Bendinat, Mallorca.

Browsing this website

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Hosting providers process technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to deliver and protect their services. This website does not install advertising or analytics trackers and does not use browser storage to save personal information.

Maps and external services

An embedded Google map is loaded only when you press “Load Google Maps”. This connects your browser to Google, which processes information under its own privacy policy. The directions link also opens Google Maps. You can use the written address without loading a map.

Email and telephone enquiries

Email links open your own email application and phone links use your device’s calling function. Nothing is sent by the website automatically. The clinic receives the information you choose to send. Please use initial enquiries for contact details and appointment preferences, and discuss sensitive medical information directly with your practitioner.

Your privacy questions

Contact the clinic to ask about information associated with your enquiry or to make a data-protection request. This notice describes the public website; ask the clinic for the privacy information applicable to patient records and treatment.

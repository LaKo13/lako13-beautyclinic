Micropigmentation in Mallorca

Discuss shape, colour and your preferences before choosing a permanent makeup treatment.

About this treatment

Micropigmentation places pigment in the skin to create cosmetic definition. The clinic’s treatment range includes brows, lips and eyeliner. A consultation is an opportunity to discuss your preferred look, the technique and how pigment can change over time.

What to discuss at your consultation

  • What shape, colour and level of definition are you looking for?
  • What training and experience does the treating practitioner have?
  • What healing, touch-up or future removal considerations should you understand?

Things to consider

Permanent makeup carries risks, including infection and allergic reactions. Removal can be difficult. Ask about the pigments, hygiene arrangements, possible colour changes and aftercare before proceeding.

Further patient information: FDA: tattoos and permanent makeup.

Planning your visit

Ask for an individual plan and quote before deciding. If you’re visiting Mallorca, mention your travel dates and allow time to discuss recovery and follow-up. Contact the clinic directly for current availability.

How do I arrange a consultation?

Call +34 971 707 725 or email info@beautyclinicmallorca.com. Let us know the treatment you’re interested in and your preferred dates. The clinic will confirm availability; an enquiry is not a confirmed appointment.

Is the first consultation free?

Yes. The clinic offers a free initial consultation. Treatment prices depend on the proposed plan; ask for an individual quote before proceeding.

Can I visit while on holiday?

We welcome both residents and visitors. Tell us your travel dates so you can discuss treatment timing, recovery and any follow-up before making plans.

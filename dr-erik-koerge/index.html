Meet Dr. Erik Koerge

More than 20 years of experience. A focus on understanding the person behind each consultation.

An individual approach to aesthetic care

With over 20 years of experience in wrinkle injections, wrinkle treatments and non-surgical facial rejuvenation, Dr. Erik Koerge takes a personal approach to aesthetic care.

Dr. Koerge’s work at Beauty Clinic Mallorca focuses on wrinkle treatments and facial rejuvenation. Consultations offer space to discuss skin quality, facial contours and the changes you would like to explore.

Bring your questions

Ask about relevant training and experience, the treatment options available to you, the products proposed and what your aftercare would involve. We encourage an informed conversation before any treatment decision.

Care in Bendinat

The clinic is located in Plaza Bendinat, close to Palma. Residents and visitors can contact us to arrange a consultation. The same contact details and opening hours apply throughout this website.
